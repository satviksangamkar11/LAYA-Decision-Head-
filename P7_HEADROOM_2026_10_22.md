# P7 step 1: selection headroom in the P6 candidate pools (2026-10-22, diagnostic, not a benchmark)

Every distinct program logged in P6 `CANDIDATES.jsonl` (v1.1 logging) was graded offline. CPU only, no new generation.
- **Contract:** 122 programs, PC, frozen contract grader, private blind set (`results/raw/p7-cand-grade-v1/contract/`).
- **Codeforces:** 1,931 programs, Kaggle CPU, grader with `stop_at_first_fail` (pass/fail only), 6 s per test (`results/raw/p7-cand-grade-v1/cf_out/`).

## Result: the selection gap is 0 on both cohorts
| Cohort | B solved | Tasks with any correct candidate (oracle@k) | Selection gap | B misses that are generation failures |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Contract D01–D20 | 16 | 16 | **0** | 4 of 4 (D08, D10, D15, D18) |
| Codeforces (72) | 8 | 8 (strict, see below) | **0** | 64 of 64 |

On both cohorts, every task with a correct candidate anywhere in the logged pool is a task B already solved. A better selector (consensus, differential
testing, the LAYA head) could not have added one correct answer on P6. The remaining loss is generation.

## Codeforces: grading-test weakness found
- **What happened:** 46 Codeforces candidates passed every grading test. When each was re-run on its problem's statement examples (`cf_hidden_pass_public_check.jsonl`),
  only 9 passed.
  - **Affected problems:** the other 37 belong to 6 problems: 2029/B, 2036/B, 2038/N, 2042/B, 2050/B, 2062/B.
  - **Example:** on 2042/B a "passing" program prints 3 3 1 where the statement's example expects 4 4 1.
- **Why:** the generated tests for those problems (26–28 each) accept wrong programs. The dataset's validation checks that correct solutions pass, not that wrong ones fail.
- **Definition used here:** strict correctness = passes the grading tests AND the statement examples. Under it, 8 tasks have a correct candidate.
  Under grading tests alone, the count would be 14, which is inflated.
- **Effect on the P6 results:** none. All of B's 8 graded passes passed the statement examples by construction (B only answers with public-passing code), and A's
  single pass (2065/A) has public_pass true. A P6 PASS can still be wrong on the complete official tests; that limitation was stated in advance.
- **Consequence for any later Codeforces claim:** grading must also require the statement examples, or use stronger tests.

## What this means for the next build
1. A P7 selection experiment on P6-like pools has no room to show a gain. Building CodeT-lite or a differential selector for this model and these tasks now is not supported.
2. The bottleneck is generation: the 1.5B model rarely writes a correct program for these problems (B: 15 answers, 8 correct; 57/72 problems with no
   public-passing sample within 6,000 tokens).
3. Candidate next directions (none started; each needs its own plan and GO):
   - generation-side changes, such as feedback from failing examples, retrieval of similar solved problems, or decomposition;
   - a stronger small model as the second model (selection room may appear where the model solves problems sometimes but not always);
   - easier task bands, where selection room exists.
